Privacy Policy

Chashma ("we") helps job seekers compare their CV with job descriptions, find vacancies and track applications. You can use it in Telegram (the @scan_cv_bot Mini App) or in a browser at app.chashma.uz. This page explains what we store, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we store

  • Account details. In Telegram: your Telegram user id, username and first name. In the browser: the name and email address from your Google account, and Google's account id. Also your chosen language.
  • Your CVs. The text extracted from the CV files you upload, and a label for each CV. We do not keep the original file.
  • What you ask us to analyse. Job descriptions you paste, and the results we generate: match scores, keyword lists, CV fixes and preparation roadmaps.
  • Your job search. Saved job title and location, vacancies you track, their status and your notes, and vacancy alerts we send you.
  • Payments. For paid packages (Telegram only, via Payme): the package, amount and payment status. We never see or store your card details. Payme handles them.
  • Usage events. Which features you use and when, for example "CV uploaded" or "analysis run". We use these to improve the product and count your free checks.
  • A sign-in cookie (browser only) that keeps you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service: run analyses, show your history, and send the vacancy alerts you switched on.
  • To count free checks and process paid packages.
  • To fix problems and improve the product.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else processes it

  • Anthropic (Claude AI). To analyse your CV, we send its text and the job description to Anthropic's Claude AI. Anthropic processes it to produce the result and does not use it to train its models.
  • Railway. Our servers and database are hosted by Railway.
  • Google. When you sign in with Google, Google confirms who you are. We only receive your name, email and Google account id.
  • Telegram. When you use the Mini App, Telegram passes us your Telegram account details.
  • Payme. Handles payments for paid packages.

Vacancies come from public job boards. We don't send them your CV or personal details.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, we delete it from our live systems within 30 days. Backup copies are removed when they are replaced, within 90 days.

Your choices

  • See your CVs, analyses and tracked applications in the app at any time.
  • Turn vacancy alerts off in the app.
  • Ask us to delete your account and all its data, or to send you a copy of it, by emailing chashmauz1@gmail.com. Tell us the Google email or Telegram username you use with Chashma.

Children

Chashma is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.
